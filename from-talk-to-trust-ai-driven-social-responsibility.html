From Talk to Trust: Cultural Dynamics of AI-Driven Social Responsibility in Negotiations for Strategic Communication

Riad Al Chami

Abstract

The increasing demand for corporate social responsibility (CSR) has fundamentally reshaped business practices, embedding ethical standards into negotiation processes and strategic communication. This study explores the cultural dynamics of AI-driven social responsibility in negotiations, focusing on how socially responsible negotiation practices influence consumer trust and brand loyalty. By integrating CSR principles into negotiation frameworks, companies can align their practices with consumer expectations, fostering transparency, accountability, and ethical integrity. Drawing on case studies of globally recognized brands such as Patagonia, Ben & Jerry's, and Unilever, the research examines the role of transparency, ethical labeling, and stakeholder engagement in building consumer trust and loyalty. The findings reveal that brands with consistent and authentic CSR practices, supported by transparent communication and ethical sourcing, achieve higher levels of consumer trust and loyalty. Conversely, inconsistent or superficial CSR efforts, as seen in cases like H&M, risk undermining consumer trust due to perceptions of "greenwashing." The study introduces a model that conceptualizes ethical negotiation practices as catalysts for enduring consumer relationships, highlighting the strategic importance of CSR in sustaining competitive advantage within values-driven markets. Furthermore, the integration of AI technologies in negotiation processes is explored, emphasizing the need for culturally adaptive frameworks that align with global consumer expectations and ethical considerations. This research contributes to the evolving discourse on CSR by offering a comprehensive framework for aligning ethical practices with consumer expectations, ultimately fostering trust, loyalty, and sustainable business growth. The study underscores the transformative potential of AI in enhancing strategic communication and negotiation, advocating for a holistic approach to CSR that balances profitability with social responsibility.
