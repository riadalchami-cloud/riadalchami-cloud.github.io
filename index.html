About

Riad Al Chami is an Assistant Professor of Marketing at the College of Business Administration, American University in the Emirates (AUE), Dubai, where he also directs the MBA program. His research examines how organizations institutionalize corporate social responsibility, how green communication can turn into greenwashing or greenbleaching, how sustainability shapes supply chains and airline marketing, and how artificial intelligence and immersive technologies change brand trust, engagement and strategic communication. His work appears in Business Strategy and the Environment, Corporate Communications: An International Journal, Journal of Global Marketing, Administrative Sciences and Innovative Marketing, and in books from Springer Nature and IGI Global.

Also published as: Riad AlChami, Riad Alchami, Riad El Chami, Riad Al-Chami.
