Integrating Sustainability in Aviation: A Comprehensive Analysis of Emirates Airlines' Strategies in Global Marketing and Consumer Dynamics

Riad Al Chami

In Navigating Business Through Essential Sustainable Strategies, pp. 137–162. IGI Global Scientific Publishing, 2025. DOI: 10.4018/979-8-3693-5360-8.ch006

Download PDFPublisher version (DOI)Cite

Published chapter (IGI Global author fair use). Final chapter PDF posted on the author's personal website under IGI Global's fair use policy for authors. Please cite and link to the published version via the DOI.

In brief. Combining Emirates Group employee views with corporate data, this chapter shows how sustainability strategy supports Emirates Airlines' global market position and customer loyalty.

Key findings

  • Emirates' sustainability strategy supports its position as a market leader in global aviation.
  • Customers notice the airline's sustainability commitment and factor it into their choice.
  • Green marketing strengthens brand loyalty, and better environmental performance goes with better economic performance.

Abstract

Emirates Airlines' sustainable initiatives in their worldwide marketing strategy, market dynamics of entry, and customer environmental concerns are examined in this study. This study will use a qualitative exploratory research design to combine Emirates Group employee views with a detailed analysis of corporate data. The statistics demonstrate Emirates Airlines' sustainability strategy, which has made them market leaders. Customers also notice this commitment to sustainability and choose that airline for it. Emirates Airlines can dominate the international aviation business by focusing on sustainability, which is less harmful to nature. Strong green marketing strategies change brand loyalty, according to data. The prior sentence emphasizes air transport environmental protection. Synthesizing eco-friendly aircraft fuels and choosing foreign technological advantages are also covered in the dissertation. The study found that sustainable airplane environmental performance improves economic performance and public participation.
