Online Shopping Consumer Behavior: Why Are We Afraid to Purchase Online? Case Study Lebanon

Riad El Chami

International Journal of Management and Commerce Innovations, 6(1), 58–71. 2018.

Download PDFCite

Published version (open access, CC BY-NC 3.0). Open access under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 3.0 licence.

In brief. This study explains why Lebanese consumers hesitate to buy online, pointing to low trust in e-commerce sites, poor website usability and high perceived risk.

Key findings

  • Lebanese consumers show low trust and confidence in online shopping sites.
  • Website usability problems and perceived purchase risk discourage online buying.

Abstract

This research covers the main factors that are affecting the decision making of the Lebanese consumer, and his behavior when purchasing a product or a service online. The result asserts that Lebanese consumers have no trust, no confidence in online sites, faces difficulties when encountering website, perceived risk of internet purchase.
