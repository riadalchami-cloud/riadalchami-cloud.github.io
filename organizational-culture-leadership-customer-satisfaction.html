Organizational Culture and Leadership Practices Impact on the Satisfaction of the Customer

Riad El Chami

In Organizational Culture in the Middle East: A Lebanese Perspective, pp. 108–135. Cambridge Scholars Publishing, 2022.

In brief. A survey of 200 senior managers in international companies in Lebanon and Dubai links employee motivation, employee involvement, leadership practices and organizational vision to customer satisfaction.

Key findings

  • Organizational culture works as a differentiation strategy that supports long-term profit, efficiency and growth.
  • Employee motivation, involvement, leadership practices and organizational vision are tested as drivers of customer satisfaction.
  • Middle Eastern organizations need to strengthen their corporate culture to raise customer satisfaction and retention.

Abstract

Organizational culture is considered as a differentiation strategy that leads to long-term profits, efficiency and growth of companies. This study will focus on organizational culture. Employee motivation, employee involvement, leadership practices, and the organizational vision are considered the independent variables and customer satisfaction is considered the dependent variable for the study. The empirical part was based on a quantitative study with a questionnaire as a survey tool filled by 200 senior managers from different levels working in international companies operating in Lebanon and Dubai. The study found that Middle Eastern organizations are in need of strengthening their corporate culture to increase customer satisfaction and their retention rate.
