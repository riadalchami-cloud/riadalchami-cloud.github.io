Significance of Marketing in School

Riad El Chami, Fadel Yakhne

International Journal of Management and Commerce Innovations, 6(1), 45–57. 2018.

Download PDFCite

Published version (open access, CC BY-NC 3.0). Open access under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 3.0 licence.

In brief. Based on interviews with admissions heads in Tripoli, Lebanon, this study identifies what drives parents' school choice and turns it into a marketing strategy for schools.

Key findings

  • Identifies the most common factors parents weigh when choosing a school.
  • Proposes a school marketing strategy built around those factors.

Abstract

The purpose of this study is to focus on the factors that influence parental decision-making when it comes to choosing a school for their beloved children. Studying the factors will give us a deep understanding of what parents look for in a school, which, in return, will help us know what a school should focus on when setting its marketing strategy. In order to achieve our goal, we have interviewed head of admission of different schools in Tripoli, and focused on the most common factors. Finally, we compared our results with different researches, and came up with a marketing strategy that every school needs to apply for a successful campaign.
